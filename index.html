Our philosophy

Seven pillars. One whole human.

Every weekly mission touches at least one pillar. Over a year, they balance out — so your child grows wide, not just tall.

  • Love & Emotional Security

    The base layer. Responsive warmth builds a brain that feels safe enough to explore.

  • Health & Fitness

    Sleep, food, movement. Small daily rhythms that protect the body for decades.

  • Intelligence & Curiosity

    Not flashcards — questions. We protect the instinct to wonder, poke and try again.

  • Character & Values

    Honesty, kindness, effort. Modelled in ordinary moments long before they’re taught.

  • Confidence & Communication

    A child who is listened to becomes a child who speaks up. We practise both sides.

  • Independence & Life Skills

    “Help me do it myself.” Age-right autonomy, from holding a spoon to holding a plan.

  • Family Culture & Relationships

    The invisible curriculum — rituals, repair after conflict, and the stories you retell.

Week 14

“Discovering Faces, Voices & Movement”

Your baby’s world is sharpening. Faces are now the most interesting thing in the room, your voice is the favourite sound, and those arms are starting to obey. This week we lean into all three.

👀 Visual tracking 🗣️ Turn-taking 💪 Neck & core

🎯 This Week’s Goals

  • Emotional

    Baby settles faster when they see or hear you — security through predictability.

  • Physical

    Holds head steady for 30+ seconds in tummy time; swipes at a dangling object.

  • Brain

    Tracks a slow-moving face or toy smoothly from one side to the other.

  • Language

    Coos back in “conversation” — waits, then answers with a sound.

  • Social

    Smiles at a familiar face on purpose, not just reflexively.

⏱️ Choose your week

Some weeks you have thirty minutes. Some weeks you have five. Both count.

Minimum5 min/day
Better15 min/day
Best30+ min/day

💌 This Week’s Missions

🌸
Mom Mission

Ten uninterrupted minutes of face-to-face time, phone in another room. Then ten minutes that are only yours — tea, shower, sleep. Your calm is part of the curriculum.

🧭
Dad Mission

Own one full routine end-to-end this week — bath, change, settle — without being asked or corrected. Different hands, different voice, same safety.

🏡
Family Mission

Create one 3-minute evening ritual everyone joins: lights low, one song, one sentence each about the day. Start it this week; keep it for years.

🚫 What to Avoid

  • Screens of any kind, including “baby-safe” videos.
  • Propping baby into a sitting position they can’t hold alone.
  • Over-stimulating toys — flashing lights plus loud sound at once.
  • Comparing your baby’s week to another baby’s week.
  • Waking a sleeping baby to complete a mission. Sleep wins.

⚠️ Red Flags

Mention these to your paediatrician — not a diagnosis, just a conversation starter.

  • No eye contact or social smile at all by 14 weeks.
  • Head still flops completely with no control when supported.
  • Doesn’t startle or turn toward loud, sudden sounds.
  • Hands stay tightly fisted all day, every day.
  • Marked loss of a skill they clearly had last month.
🔬 Evidence-backed

Science Corner

Around three to four months, infants shift from reflexive looking to voluntary visual tracking, and the “serve and return” rhythm of adult–infant babble becomes measurable. Studies of contingent responding — replying right after your baby vocalises, rather than talking over them — show richer babble and stronger later vocabulary.

Take-away: the pause is the intervention. Wait three seconds, then answer.
